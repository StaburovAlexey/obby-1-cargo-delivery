## Scope and limitations

- **driver:** Deterministic clean bridge-aware controller, not fastest possible or human-play timing. Holds/releases ordinary pedal only; requests about 4 m/s near narrow bridges and future unsuitable terrain; no steering/pose/fuel injection.
- **refills:** Free and instantaneous when the actual vehicle centre enters each prior right-hand 4.4 m fuel circle; every prior station required by the harness. No dwell time added. Successful delivery to the left-hand circle instantly pays and returns to hangar.
- **restart:** Driving-only timing excludes garage walking, cooldown, car purchase, menus and reload. Cash/min shown separately with assumed 0, 5, 10 and 20 seconds of restart overhead per run. Those overheads are sensitivities, not measurements.
- **upgrades:** Stock means all levels 0; max10 means all four stats level 10 already owned. Does not charge time or brains to buy upgrades, and does not assert an optimal purchase plan.
- **failures:** All reported successful rates assume no missed refills, collision or bridge fall. Real learning and mistakes are unmeasured. Late failed runs pay zero, so real long-route yield can be substantially lower.
- **reward:** One endpoint payout per run, not cumulative station rewards. No skill bonuses exist in this baseline. Driving brains measured through production tickProgress are separate from delivery money.
- **scope:** Offline CPU production modules at 20 Hz controller update and 120 Hz fixed physics. No browser/GPU/rendering or human playtest claim.

- The selected endpoint for each vehicle is the existing balance harness target, freshly driven here. This is not an exhaustive proof that no other route/line can reach farther.
- The controller runs at 20 Hz (0.05 s), within the application’s 0.05 s simulation-delta cap; physics uses its production fixed step of 1/120 s. This differs from the historical expanded-balance harness’s 0.1 s input sampling. All movement is generated by production model controls.
- Upgrade brains are pooled separately from delivery money. Production driving earns about one brain per moving second. Tank10 for the final car costs 3,350 brains. The max-all rows do not include upgrade acquisition time.
- Human error, menus, walking, failures and learning are unmeasured. The small starter slalom check is not an estimate of player failure rate. Do not label these as average-player or speedrun times.

## Important calibration risks

- **Income inversion near the first bridge:** stock citycourier at stop3 earns 444.8/min; its max-all stop4 route earns 372.9/min. Newer/more distant objectives are not automatically better earning loops.
- **Fragile snowrunner stock endpoint:** stop12 takes 746.75 s, pays 8,460 and leaves only 1.506/880 fuel, about 4.8 m of suitable-terrain reserve. Even a small extra final-leg path may erase an entire 12.4-minute payout. Snowgrip stock stop9 also has a modest 12.312/650 reserve, about 34.1 m.
- **Short-farm sensitivity:** ash max stop3 earns 1,317.8/min driving-only, 1,068.2/min at +5 s restart and 898.1/min at +10 s. Its full route earns 1,009.0/min driving-only and 1,000.4/min at +10 s. Approximate crossover between these measured loops is 6.71 s restart overhead.
